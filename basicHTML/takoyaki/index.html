Takoyaki

Takoyaki is a Japanese snack of little balls stuffed with boiled octopus.

Ingredients

  • Octopus Sashimi
  • Pre-made batter
  • Tempura Scraps
  • Bonito Flakes
  • Kewpie Mayo>

Instructions

  1. Make the batter
  2. Pour batter into pan with round chambers
  3. Add octopus and tempura scraps
  4. Flip once bottom is crisp
  5. Once cooked, drizzle flakes and mayo
